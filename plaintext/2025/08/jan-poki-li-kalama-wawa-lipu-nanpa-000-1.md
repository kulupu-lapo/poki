### lipu lili nanpa tu wan:
**[jan Poki]** kulupu jan pi kalama musi... la jan pi pilin ike kin li ken pana e pilin ona  
[monsi: kulupu jan pi kalama wawa - musi [len] pi [len] luka luka tu tu]  
**[jan kalama]** tomo sona sewi la mi jan pi pilin ike li lon monsi tomo, li lukin e lipu  
**[jan pi alasa sona]** aaa, wawa a!?  
**[jan pi alasa sona]** taso, tenpo ni la, sina lon kulupu ni: jan lili mute li sona e sina a!  
**[jan kalama]** a... lon...  
**[jan Poki]** kulupu kalama a...  
[sona: jan Koto Itoli: sike nanpa wan pi tomo sona insa]

![lipu lili #4](https://dl.dropboxusercontent.com/scl/fi/un1i78dc889e9jf8r7yql/4.png?rlkey=4kfj9gcte6o2tkfzs47y2anav&st=0cz48tba&dl=0)
### lipu lili nanpa tu tu:
**[jan Poki]** *kulupu kalama la jan pi pilin ike sama kin li ken pana e ona anu seme...*  
**[jan Poki]** m- mi ken ala ken jo e ilo kalama sina?  
**[mama mije]** aaa, pini la, meli lili mi kin li wile sona e ilo kalama linja...  
[ilo kalama linja la, mu: suno...]  
**[jan Poki]** *po... pona a*  
**[jan Poki]** *ni li ken wile e pali mute. taso, mi alasa kama sona e nasin kalama pona*  
**[jan Poki]** E? A? G?  
**[jan Poki]** tan seme la, toki Inli li lon...?

![lipu lili #5](https://dl.dropboxusercontent.com/scl/fi/6ra1snakw9zdkzv40f3ez/5.png?rlkey=sdheuqy0mw2qt8wtwypixm11o&st=5frsrv8v&dl=0)
### lipu lili nanpa luka:
**[jan Poki]** *ona li kalama nasa...*  
**[jan Poki]** *mi ken ala ni a!*  
[mu: kalama suli]  
**[jan Poki]** *n... mi awen alasa lili*  
**[jan Poki]** *ona o sama ni anu seme?*  
[mu: wawa a...]  
**[mama meli]** jan Itoli li kalama mute e ilo kalama lon tenpo poka a?  
**[mama mije]** wile mi la, kalama musi pi ma weka li pona tawa ona kin  
[mu: mu ilo, mu ilo ... mu ilo, mu ilo ... mu ilo wawa, mu ilo wawa]  
**[mama mije]** *nimi pi soweli mi li tan jan pi kalama musi weka*  
**[jan Poki]** *tenpo sike tu li pini tan ni: mi open kama sona tawa tenpo suli lon suno ale*  
[*monsi:

**[jan Poki]** *tenpo poka la, jan mute li kute e kalama mi, li toki pona tawa mi...*  
**[jan Poki]** *mi wile kalama lon tenpo poka. taso... mi ken ala ken kalama tawa jan ante mute...*  
**[jan Poki]** *kin la, mi jo ala e jan pona...*  
**[jan Poki]** *mi ken kalama lon tenpo musi pi nasin jan la, mi kama sona tawa jan ale...*

### lipu lili nanpa luka wan:
**[jan sona]** ni la, jan awen mi pi kama sona li kalama musi tawa jan weka mi pi kama sona  
**[jan awen pi kama sona]** "sina nanpa wan tawa mi"  
**[jan awen pi kama sona]** "jan pi pana sona o"  
**[jan Poki]** ...  
**[jan Poki]** *n. sona ala mi la, tomo sona insa li pini a*  
[namako la, toki ante li kalama musi Akepa Totusi (*kalama musi* "sina nanpa wan tawa mi"). ona li toki e pini pi tenpo sona.]  
**[jan Poki]** *tenpo sike tu wan la, mi awen jo ala e jan pona...*  
**[jan Poki]** *mi kama jo ala e jan pi kulupu kalama...*  
**[jan Poki]** *mi kalama ala lon tenpo musi pi nasin jan...*  
**[jan Poki]** *tomo sona sewi...*  
**[jan Poki]** *mi o kalama lon kulupu kalama lon tomo sona sewi...*
